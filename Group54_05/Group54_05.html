5. Issues

  1. Issue: SSL/TLS certificate verification failures occurred on CMake setup when attempting to download SFML packages over secure connections on restricted networks.
  2. Resolution: Configured the CMake run parameters to skip TLS certificate verification (CMAKE_TLS_VERIFY=OFF) since the SFML archive is fetched from trusted sources.
  3. Issue: Mouse click events from the main menu were leaking through and instantly selecting characters or state options on the succeeding screen.
  4. Resolution: Added an input delay timer (m_timeInState) inside the update loop of transition states to ignore click registers during the first 200ms.
  5. Issue: Axe, Magic Wand, Knife, and Whip projectile textures were drawing upside down or mapping to wrong sprite offsets due to differences in coordinate spaces.
  6. Resolution: Re-mapped the source rectangle coordinates (sf::IntRect) based on top-left offsets matching the target spritesheets.
